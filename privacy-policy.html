1. Overview

Cruz Financial Associates LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who interact with our business, website, and communication channels. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or engage with our services.

By using our website or contacting us, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with Cruz Financial Associates LLC:

  • Contact information — name, phone number, email address, and mailing address provided voluntarily when you reach out to us
  • Property information — details about real estate assets you share with us in connection with a potential transaction
  • Communication records — records of calls, text messages, and written correspondence, including those conducted via telephone or SMS for business purposes
  • Usage data — browser type, IP address, pages visited, and time spent on our website, collected automatically via standard web technologies
  • Transaction data — financial and deal-related information shared in the course of consulting or acquisition services

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To evaluate, structure, and facilitate real estate transactions and consulting engagements
  • To communicate with you regarding deals, services, or inquiries you have initiated
  • To conduct outbound prospecting and marketing calls or messages in compliance with applicable law
  • To maintain business records and comply with legal obligations
  • To improve our website and business operations
  • To protect against fraud and unauthorized activity

4. Telephone and SMS Communications

Cruz Financial Associates LLC uses telephone and SMS-based communication systems, including automated or power-dialed outreach, to contact property owners, buyers, and business prospects. By providing your phone number to us or responding to our outreach, you consent to receiving calls and/or text messages from Cruz Financial Associates LLC related to our real estate and financial services.

Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message. To opt out of calls, notify us directly at +1 (860) 212-7882.

We do not use automated communications for unlawful purposes and maintain internal do-not-contact lists in compliance with applicable telemarketing regulations including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA).

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With transaction parties — buyers, sellers, title companies, attorneys, or other parties necessary to complete a real estate deal

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations. Transaction records may be retained for a minimum of five (5) years. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us directly, subject to our legal retention requirements.
